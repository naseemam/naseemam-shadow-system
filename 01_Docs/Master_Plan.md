# Master Plan

## Purpose

The Master Plan is the central roadmap for Project Ameer.

It defines the long-term vision, development phases, priorities, milestones, and overall progress of the project.

This document serves as the primary planning reference for the founder and guides the evolution of Ameer from concept to a fully operational personal AI companion.

It is updated whenever major decisions, completed milestones, or strategic changes occur.

The Constitution remains the highest authority for all project decisions.
